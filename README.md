# Elemental Mirror

Fire, ice, water, lightning and magic from your own hands and mouth, live in your browser through your webcam.

**Try it:** open the link on this repository's Pages site in Chrome or Edge on a computer, click **Start camera**, and allow the camera.

## What you can do

- Give each hand its own element, and choose where it comes out: palm, index fingertip, every fingertip, or the whole hand.
- Open your mouth to breathe an element out, and turn your head to aim it. Purse your lips to blow air on a flame.
- Hold a fireball, water ball or magic ball on your palm. Feed it with your other hand, flick to throw it, or push it at the camera to smash it on the screen.
- Freeze a hand, then breathe fire on it to melt the ice.
- Swap the room behind you for another scene or your own picture, with adjustable blur and brightness.
- Record a clip to share.

## How it works

Everything runs on your own computer. Hand, face and body tracking use Google's MediaPipe models, and the fire, mist, smoke and water are a fluid simulation running on the graphics card with WebGL 2. The whole project is one file, `index.html`.

## Privacy

Video and sound are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.

## Requirements

A computer with a webcam and a recent version of Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari may work but are untested.
